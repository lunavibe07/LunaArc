LunaArc packages the unmodified 7-Zip 26.04 command-line encoder.
Copyright (C) 1999-2026 Igor Pavlov.

Windows: 7zr.exe (renamed LunaArc.7zr.exe).
Linux x64: 7zz (renamed LunaArc.7zz).
This is a separate executable; LunaArc does not link it into its assemblies.
The encoder can be replaced using the LUNAARC_7ZIP_PATH environment variable.

7-Zip home: https://www.7-zip.org/
Exact release and corresponding source:
